"Programming is solved"

Posted on: Wed 29 July 2026

Recently it is common to hear things like: "Programming is Solved", "There is no need to learn how to code anymore", etc.

Saying "AI" can program better than most people, so there is no need to learn programming anymore is the equivalent of saying calculators do arithmetic better than most people, so there is no need to learn arithmetic anymore.

The problem with this line of thinking is multifolds:

  • "AI" or more accurately multimodal large langage models are a tool to enhance our abilities. Not a replacement.
  • We need to be careful about people trying to oversell their product (CEOs of "AI" companies aiming at overhyped IPOs)
  • Blindly believing those people will likely lead to blindly relying on "AI" models, forgetting that those models are not coming up with things by themselves (as hard as people are trying to sell otherwise). "AI" models learn from humans output. Thus, people stopping to learn programming will lead to a situation where there will only be a few capable programmers that can train the "AI" models from their skills. This will end-up in a situation where those very "AI" model will stagnate overtime.

It is also common to ear some people saying that all programmers are now equal thanks to "AI". Ok, consider a primary school student with a scientific calculator in the hand of a primary school student vs a scientific calculator in the hand of a math university student. Is the primary school student as competent as the university math student? I don't think so. The primary student will not even know how to use most of the features of the scientific calculator simply because they don't understand the concept themselves.

Let's be careful about blindly believing in wanna-be "AI" overlords, who unfortunately started believing in their own delusion.

"Science sans conscience n'est que ruine de l'ame" -- François Rabelais

Use "AI" models with the right mindset.
